LockIn Privacy Policy

Zero-Storage Cash-Flow Auditing

1. Read-Only, Bounded Access

LockIn requests strictly read-only email access (gmail.readonly or Mail.Read) to query receipt, invoice, subscription, and statement emails from the last 90 days (capped at 50 messages per scan). LockIn never sends, modifies, or deletes emails, and never requests bank credentials.

2. In-Memory Processing & PII Redaction

Email text is processed exclusively in volatile memory (RAM). Before structured bill extraction, a local regex pass strips personal identifiers such as email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, and partial card/account numbers.

3. Zero Database Persistence

LockIn uses no database. OAuth access tokens exist only in browser memory for the active scan, and neither tokens nor email contents are written to disk or application logs. Closing the tab or clicking Clear immediately discards your session.
